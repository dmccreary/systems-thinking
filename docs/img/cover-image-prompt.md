# Cover Image Prompt

Please generate a professional-quality cover image for this textbook.
This image will be used in social media previews and must follow the
formatting guidelines for an Open Graph image preview.

**Required specifications:**

- Format: PNG
- Wide-landscape format
- Size: 1200x630 pixels (1.91:1 aspect ratio)
- This is the Open Graph standard for social media previews

The image has four layers, back to front: background montage, color
treatment, mascot, and title text.

**Reference image:** attach `docs/img/mascot/welcome.png` along with this
prompt. It is the exact mascot character that must appear in the lower-left
corner.

## Subject & Tone

**Systems Thinking in the Age of AI** is an interactive textbook about seeing
the whole system: feedback loops, stocks and flows, systems archetypes,
leverage points, and emergence. It applies these ideas to technology,
including graph databases, enterprise knowledge graphs, breaking down
organizational silos, and the feedback dynamics of AI and machine learning.
Readers range from 8th grade and high school students to college and graduate
students, IT leaders, business executives, and government agency staff.

The visual tone should be **modern, optimistic, and intellectually inviting**.
It should look technical enough for an IT executive and friendly enough for a
junior high student. It should feel like a map of connected ideas, not a
corporate slide.

## Title

Place the title in the center of the image on two lines:

- Line 1, large and bold: **Systems Thinking**
- Line 2, about 60% of the size of line 1: **in the Age of AI**

Use a clean, highly legible geometric sans-serif font. Render line 1 in white
and line 2 in warm orange (#F5871F). Put a soft dark scrim or subtle drop
shadow behind the text so it stays readable against the montage. Keep the
area directly behind the title calm and uncluttered. The title block should
span roughly the middle 60% of the width (about x = 240 to 960 px) and sit
slightly above the vertical center.

Spell the title exactly as written above. Add no other words to the image.

## Background Montage

Arrange the following 9 concepts in a loose ring around the title. Draw every
element in the same flat vector style (see Style below) so the composition
reads as one connected picture, not a collage. Link neighboring elements with
thin, glowing curved arrows so the whole montage looks like one big causal
loop diagram.

1. **Reinforcing and balancing feedback loops**: two circular arrow loops side
   by side, one tagged with a small "R" badge and one with a small "B" badge,
   with tiny "+" and "−" signs at the arrowheads.
2. **Stocks and flows**: a simple bathtub with a faucet pouring water in at
   the top and a drain letting water out at the bottom, with the water level
   at about half full.
3. **The leverage points iceberg**: an iceberg with a small tip above a wavy
   waterline and a much larger mass below, shaded in bands that get darker
   with depth (light aqua near the surface, deep teal, then navy).
4. **Seven Bridges of Königsberg**: a stylized top-down river map with two
   islands and seven small bridges, with a dotted overlay turning the land
   masses into circular nodes and the bridges into edges. This marks the
   birth of graph theory.
5. **Enterprise knowledge graph**: a glowing network of round nodes with
   directed edges between them, in several colors, with one hub node brighter
   than the rest.
6. **Breaking down organizational silos**: three or four tall grain-silo
   towers standing in a row, with bright network lines bridging across the
   gaps between them. One silo wall is cracking open with light spilling out.
7. **Neural network**: three columns of circular nodes (input, hidden, output)
   connected by orange weighted lines of varying thickness.
8. **Growth and chaos**: a bifurcation diagram, a single line that forks into
   two, then four, then dissolves into a fine spray of dots. Next to it, a
   smooth S-shaped logistic growth curve.
9. **AI flywheel**: a circular flywheel of four arrows (data → better model →
   more users → more data) around a small glowing chip icon.

## Mascot

Place the book's mascot, **Sage the Owl**, in the **lower-left corner** of the
image. Reproduce the attached reference image (`welcome.png`) as faithfully as
possible: same character, same pose, same colors, same proportions. Do not
restyle or reinterpret it.

- **Position:** the owl's feet about 30–40 px above the bottom edge and its
  left side about 30–40 px from the left edge.
- **Size:** about 40–45% of the image height (roughly 250–280 px tall).
- **Clearance:** the owl must sit entirely to the left of and below the title
  block, never overlapping any title letters. Keep montage elements from
  overlapping the owl. Add a soft glow or light vignette behind Sage so the
  mascot stands out from the dark background.

If the reference image cannot be attached, draw Sage from this description:
a small, round, friendly cartoon owl about two heads tall with a slate-blue
body (#2E6DA4), darker blue wing and tail feather tips, and two pointed ear
tufts. Sage has a cream-colored belly with a scalloped feather pattern, large
dark round eyes with white highlights, round orange-framed glasses (#F5871F),
a small orange beak, and orange feet with dark gray talons. The owl's right
wing (on the viewer's left) is raised in a friendly wave, with three short
orange motion lines above it. Sage wears a warm, closed-mouth smile and is
drawn in flat vector cartoon style with clean lines and bold simple shapes.

## Style & Composition

- **Illustration style:** flat vector illustration with clean lines, bold
  simple shapes, and subtle soft glows on the arrows and network edges. Use
  this style for every montage element so the mascot (also flat vector) looks
  native to the scene rather than pasted on.
- **Color palette:** a deep midnight-navy background (about #0F1B2D), with
  slate blue (#2E6DA4) and teal (#2AA7A1) as the main montage colors and warm
  orange (#F5871F) as the accent color. The palette deliberately matches
  Sage's colors.
- **Lighting/mood:** bright and optimistic. Use a gentle radial glow behind
  the title, as if the whole system is lighting up once you zoom out and see
  it.
- **Composition:** the title is centered. Montage elements form a loose ring
  around it, connected by curved glowing arrows. Sage sits in the lower-left
  corner. Leave generous negative space directly behind the title. Keep the
  title and mascot inside the central safe area so nothing important is
  cropped when LinkedIn, Slack, or X trims the edges of the preview.

## Avoid

- **No text anywhere except the title.** The only other characters allowed
  are "R", "B", "+", "−", and arrows. Do not add labels to montage elements.
  AI-rendered small words tend to come out misspelled.
- Do not render dense paragraphs or illegible text anywhere in the image.
- Avoid generic stock-photo clichés: handshakes, isolated lightbulbs, gears
  with no meaning, people pointing at whiteboards, or glowing robot heads.
- Avoid photorealistic human faces.
- Do not let montage elements overlap or compete with the title or the
  mascot.
- Do not add a second mascot, and do not change Sage's colors, glasses, or
  pose.
