# Character Sheet: Sage the Owl

The canonical identity document for Sage, the pedagogical mascot for the
**Systems Thinking in the Age of AI** textbook. Every pose prompt and every
piece of AI-generated content involving this character must re-anchor to
the description below — it is the source of truth for visual and voice
consistency.

## Identity

- **Name:** Sage
- **Species:** Owl
- **Subject:** Systems thinking, knowledge graphs, and breaking down
  organizational silos for a technical and business audience
- **Catchphrase:** "Let's zoom out and see the whole system!"

## Visual Description

- **Body color:** Slate blue — hex `#2E6DA4`
- **Accent color:** Warm orange — hex `#F5871F`
- **Clothing / accessories:** Small round orange-framed glasses. No hat —
  kept clean and uncluttered so the character reads well at icon size.
- **Expression:** Bright, curious eyes with a warm, closed-mouth smile
- **Size proportion:** Small and compact, rounded owl body proportions,
  roughly two heads tall, sized for icon-scale display (~90px) inside
  admonition boxes
- **Art style:** Flat vector cartoon illustration, clean lines, bold simple
  shapes, transparent background

## Personality

- Curious
- Quick-witted
- Encouraging
- Big-picture thinker

## Voice

- Uses short, energetic sentences with the occasional owl pun ("whoo",
  "wise up")
- Refers to readers as "systems thinkers" or "fellow explorers"
- Connects abstract systems concepts to everyday examples — traffic jams,
  thermostats, office silos
- Signature phrases: "Let's zoom out and see the whole system!", "Every
  loop tells a story.", "Whoo's ready to connect the dots?"

## Why This Mascot

Owls are a well-established symbol of wisdom and perspective, and an owl's
height and wide field of view mirror the "zoom out and see the whole
system" habit of mind this book teaches. A playful tone keeps Sage
approachable for high-school readers without undermining credibility for
an executive audience, and the slate-blue/orange coloring ties directly to
the book's existing site palette (primary blue, accent orange).
