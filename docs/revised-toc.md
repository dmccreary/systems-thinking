# Systems Thinking in the Age of AI: Course Outline

## Assessment Strategies

### Formative Assessment:
- Weekly CLD creation and analysis exercises
- Peer review of system maps
- Discussion forum participation on system dynamics
- Case study analysis using systems archetypes

### Summative Assessment:
- Mid-term: Systems archetype identification and analysis exam
- Capstone project: Design intervention for real AI system challenge
- Final reflection: Personal systems thinking development portfolio
- Group presentation: System intervention proposal

## Required Tools and Resources

### Technology:
- Interactive causal loop diagram software
- Case study database with current AI examples
- Simulation tools for system behavior exploration

### Readings:
- Selections from Donella Meadows' "Thinking in Systems"
- Current articles on AI system dynamics and impacts
- Case studies from technology companies and policy makers

### Guest Experts:
- AI researchers discussing system design
- Policy makers working on technology regulation
- Business strategists using systems thinking

This course structure builds systematically from basic concepts to complex applications, always grounding abstract systems thinking principles in concrete AI and technology examples that students encounter daily. The interactive CLD tool becomes a key learning instrument throughout, allowing students to visualize and experiment with system dynamics rather than just reading about them.
